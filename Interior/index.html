About the Studio

An atelier for quiet luxury and considered living

Maison Élan is a multidisciplinary interior studio led by principal designer Aria Mehta. We blend European editorial sensibility with warm Indian craftsmanship.

From single-room transformations to full villas, boutique offices and retail — every project begins with how you live, work and gather. We then layer architecture, light, texture and art into spaces that age beautifully.
